Foldling Paper Pet: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Foldling Paper Pet is a small game about a paper pet, made and run by Rodrigo Motta Mendes, Estonia ("we"). This page says what the app does with your data. Contact: flokigamessupport@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, your e-mail or your contacts.
  • Your game is saved on your phone only. We have no server and we never receive it.
  • The game is free. You can buy optional paper packs. You buy them through Apple's App Store, and we never see your payment details.
  • Nothing is sent to us. The app's only connection is to Apple's App Store, for the packs. The game plays without the internet.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

What the app stores

The app saves your game: your Foldling, its garden, the visitors, the album with the photos you took of your visitors inside the game, the letters, and small notes such as which tips you have already seen. It also saves a few small pictures for the widget, so the widget can show how Foldling is doing. Beside your saved game, a small file keeps which paper packs this phone owns, with Apple's id of each purchase. Nothing else.

The app reads your phone's clock (for the sky and bedtime) and, while it is open, the angle of the hinge and the tilt of the phone (for the pop-up page and the pinwheel). It uses them only inside the app and sends them nowhere. It never uses your camera, your microphone, your photos or your location.

Where it is stored

Only on your phone, inside the app's own storage (the widget's pictures in storage the app shares with its own widget). Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include it, like any app's data. Deleting the app deletes the game and the small file about your packs. It does not delete your purchases: Apple keeps those.

What is sent

Nothing is sent to us, and nothing to anyone but Apple's App Store. We have no server.

The app's only connection is to Apple's App Store, through Apple's own StoreKit. The app uses it for three things: to show the paper packs and their prices, to buy a pack, and to check which packs your Apple Account owns (when the app starts, and when you tap Restore purchases). That talk is between your phone and Apple. Apple's Privacy Policy covers it.

The game plays without the internet. Only buying a pack or Restore purchases needs it.

The Privacy policy and Help rows in the game (in the cogwheel card) open this website in your phone's web browser. The app itself sends nothing to this website; the browser loads the page as it loads any other website.

Paper packs and payments

Foldling has optional paper packs: Lantern Festival, Tea Garden, Storybook Cottage, and All Three Packs together. They are in-app purchases, bought with real money through Apple's App Store. Each is bought once and stays yours. The rest of the game is free. Scraps, the game's own coins, are only earned in the game and are never sold.

You pay in Apple's own purchase sheet, outside the game. We never see your card, your Apple Account, your name or any payment details.

Apple keeps the purchase itself. So Restore purchases brings your packs back after a new install, or on a new phone with the same Apple Account.

Why the App Store says "Data Not Collected"

On the App Store, the app's privacy label says "Data Not Collected". Here is why, in our own words. By Apple's rules for privacy labels, payment details that you enter in Apple's own purchase sheet, and that the developer never sees, are not collected by the developer. And a developer does not list what Apple itself collects. The app sends nothing to us or to a partner, so it collects nothing.

No ads, no tracking, no accounts

The app shows no ads, does not track you, uses no analytics and needs no account or sign-in.

Children

Foldling is for all ages. It does not collect any personal information from anyone, children included.

Each buy goes through Apple's own purchase sheet, so Apple's settings guard it: Face ID or the Apple Account password, and Ask to Buy for children in a Family Sharing group.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing we can look up, correct or delete. Your game is on your phone: deleting the app deletes it. Your purchases are Apple's records, not ours: for those, please ask Apple. Questions: flokigamessupport@gmail.com.

If you live in the European Union you can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).
