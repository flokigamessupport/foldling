Foldling Paper Pet: Help

Foldling Paper Pet is a little paper pet that lives in a pop-up paper garden on your phone. It follows your phone's real clock: it plays in the day, sleeps at night and is happy to see you when you come back.

Write to us

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game: flokigamessupport@gmail.com. We read every message.

How to play

  • On iPhone Duo the hinge is the game: open the phone slowly and the garden rises piece by piece; lay it flat to see the whole page; fold it shut to close the book.
  • On an iPhone that does not fold, the page pops up by itself when you open the app (a tap opens it at once). Swipe up to lay the page flat, swipe down to fold it back up. Leaving the app is the same as folding the phone shut.
  • Tap Foldling to pat it. With the page flat, tap a + spot to choose a garden piece for your paper scraps.
  • A visitor may come for a piece you placed while the app is closed. Tap it to take its photo for your album.

The berry bubble and the catch round

When Foldling gets hungry, a berry shows in its thought bubble. Tap the bubble to start the catch round: drag the basket under the berries falling from the trees. Golden ones are worth more; leave the brown spotty ones. Foldling eats its catch, and each berry it eats gives you paper scraps for the garden.

Bedtime and sleep

  • From 20:30 Foldling gets ready for bed. Fold the phone shut (or leave the app) to tuck it into its little house.
  • Still awake at midnight, it falls asleep by itself. It sleeps until 7:00 in the morning, so at night there is little to do: come back in the morning, when a folded paper gift may be waiting.
  • In the day, when it is very tired, it takes a nap and wakes up by itself once it has rested.

The widget and StandBy

Add Foldling's widget to your Home Screen (touch and hold an empty spot, then add a widget) to see how Foldling is doing while you are away. In StandBy (your iPhone charging, locked and on its side) the widget keeps you company through the night.

My Foldling is gone

Your Foldling is saved on your phone only: we have no server, so we cannot bring it back from our side. Deleting the app deletes it. A backup of your phone (for example iCloud Backup) made while Foldling was there may include it, like any app's data, so restoring that backup may bring your Foldling back.

Your paper packs are kept by Apple: Restore purchases brings them back.

Paper packs

You can buy optional paper packs: Lantern Festival, Tea Garden, Storybook Cottage (a new little house Foldling can move into), and All Three Packs together. They are in-app purchases, bought with real money through Apple's App Store. You buy each one once and it stays yours. The rest of the game is free. Paper scraps are only earned in the game; they are never sold.

How to buy a pack:

  • Lay the phone flat. On an iPhone that does not fold, swipe up on the garden.
  • Tap a dashed + spot to open the scrap box.
  • Scroll its strip to the end: the packs come after the free pieces.
  • Tap a pack to see its card, then tap Buy. Apple's own sheet asks you to confirm.

The pack's pieces then wait in the scrap box, ready to place. Storybook Cottage is a house: once it is yours, choose it in the scrap box and Foldling moves in.

Each pack also brings a garden view with its own sky, ground and music: Lantern Festival brings Lantern town, Tea Garden brings Tea hills, Storybook Cottage brings Storybook fields, and All Three Packs brings all three. Once you own one, a Views tab shows by the Album tab (top left): tap it to choose a view, or Classic for the free garden.

You pay in Apple's own sheet, so we never see your card or any payment details. Buying a pack needs the internet; the game itself plays without it.

Restore purchases

Apple keeps your purchases. So after a new install, or on a new phone, you can bring your packs back: open the cogwheel card (top right) and tap the Restore purchases row. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. It needs the internet.

The game then says "Your packs are back!" or "Nothing to restore". If Apple does not answer, the game says so and you can try again later. Nothing is lost by a failed try.

Family Sharing

The packs can be shared through Apple's Family Sharing when your family's Purchase Sharing is on. A family member opens Foldling and taps Restore purchases.

Refunds

Payments go through Apple, so Apple handles refunds, not us. To ask for one:

  • Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Choose "Request a refund" and pick the reason.
  • Choose the item and submit.

If Apple refunds a pack, its pieces go back to the shop.

Privacy

The app has no accounts, shows no ads, sends nothing to us and keeps your game on your phone only. Buying goes through Apple. Read the Privacy Policy.
